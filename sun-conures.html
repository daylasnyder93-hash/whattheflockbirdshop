Rex is a Red-Fronted Amazon with a big personality, a bigger appetite, and absolutely zero interest in pretending he’s not in charge. His sex hasn't been confirmed, but honestly, Rex has never let that stop him from having an opinion.

This dude spends an impressive amount of his time in the food bowl. If there's food in front of him, Rex will be there. If you refill the bowl, he'll be there. If you dare walk past the bowl without offering something, he'll probably have a few words about it.

Rex is definitely all attitude with a tiny bit of sweet mixed in. He's not afraid to throw a bite at most people, but once he decides you're alright, he'll happily love you... from a comfortable distance.

Basically, Rex is a food-loving little menace who knows exactly what he wants — and isn't afraid to let you know it.
