Simple In-House Financing — 0% Interest

Looking for another way to make bringing home your new feathered friend more manageable? We also offer in-house financing with no interest!

A 50% down payment is required to reserve your bird, and the remaining balance may be paid over a period of up to 120 days. Make payments at your own pace during that period — there are no interest charges or financing fees.

Your feathered friend will remain in our care and cannot leave until the balance has been paid in full.

The entire remaining balance must be paid within 120 days of the initial deposit. If the balance is not paid in full by the end of the 120-day period, all payments made toward the bird will be forfeited and are non-refundable, and the bird will no longer be held for the purchaser.

By selecting our in-house financing option, the purchaser agrees to these payment and reservation terms.
