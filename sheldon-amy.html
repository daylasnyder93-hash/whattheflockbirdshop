Meet Sheldon & Amy—our African grey pair proving that even the nerdiest love story can get a little feathery. 🦜🤓

Sheldon is fairly certain he’s the smartest bird in the building. That’s HIS perch, you’re standing in HIS spot, and any changes to the feeding schedule should have been submitted in writing three business days ago.

Amy has the brains, the patience, and apparently the clearance required to access Sheldon’s personal space. Their romance started with a strict relationship agreement and has now progressed to three eggs. Apparently, somebody approved an amendment. 🥚🥚🥚

Here at What the Flock Bird Shop, we provide the food, clean the house, and pay the bills while these two work on producing the next generation of tiny feathered know-it-alls.

Love is in the air. So is a detailed list of everything we’re doing wrong. Bazinga!
