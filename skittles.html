Meet Skittles, the only bird at What the Flock Bird Shop who looks like he fell into a paint factory and came out with an attitude problem.

Skittles believes rules are merely suggestions, personal space is a myth, and if something isn’t covered in fruit juice, you’re clearly not having enough fun.

He’s loud, colorful, ridiculously energetic, and has the table manners of a raccoon at an all-you-can-eat buffet.

His hobbies include redecorating with nectar, judging everyone who walks by, and turning a freshly cleaned cage into a crime scene in under 30 seconds.

🌈 Skittles: Taste the rainbow. Fear the aftermath.
