Bird Sales Terms & Conditions

At What The Flock Bird Shop, our birds come first. The following terms explain our policies regarding deposits, purchases, pickup, and the care and representation of our birds.

Deposits

A deposit may be required to reserve a bird.

All deposits are non-refundable. The only exception is if the reserved bird becomes unfit for sale due to illness, injury, or another condition that materially affects the bird's health or suitability for placement.

In such cases, the buyer may elect to receive a full refund of the deposit or apply the deposit immediately toward another available bird of the buyer's choice.

Payment & Pickup

The balance of the purchase price must be paid in full before the bird is released to the buyer, unless otherwise agreed to in writing.

The agreed-upon pickup/delivery date will be established at the time of sale.

Transfer of Ownership

Upon transfer of possession, the buyer assumes responsibility for the bird's care, housing, nutrition, supervision, and veterinary needs.

Bird Characteristics & Representations

The buyer acknowledges that birds are living animals and may exhibit individual behaviors, temperaments, and characteristics that cannot be guaranteed by the Seller.

Any representations regarding a bird's age, sex, species, or health are made to the best of the Seller's knowledge at the time of sale.

Seller's Right to Refuse or Cancel a Sale

What The Flock Bird Shop reserves the sole right to refuse or cancel a sale and refund any deposit or payments received if there is reasonable cause to believe that the bird would not be placed in a safe, appropriate, or humane environment, or if the placement would otherwise be contrary to the bird's welfare.

Agreement

This agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and may only be modified in writing signed by both parties.
